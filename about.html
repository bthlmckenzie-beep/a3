Our Story

Made because socks shouldn't go thin at the heel

We're a small, independent studio — not a large manufacturer. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A dress sock that lasted one month

Hosiery Drawer started with a specific frustration: a dress sock that looked sharp for the first few wears and then developed a hole right at the heel. We started testing cotton and wool blends ourselves, checking how they actually held up after real daily wear and repeated washing.

Every pair since has been built around that same reinforced standard.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every pair

01

Fabric tested through real washes

We wash swatches dozens of times before committing to a blend.

02

Reinforced where it matters

Heel and toe reinforcement is standard, not an upgrade.

03

Small batches, checked by hand

Every batch gets a hands-on quality check before it ships.
